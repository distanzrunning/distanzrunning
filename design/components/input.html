Input

A single-line text field. Sits on surface (#fff) with a hairline gray-alpha-400 ring; base radius 6px. Focus is a grey halo (--ds-focus-border) — the grey form-control treatment, not the blue accent, which is reserved for actionable elements. Four sizes, plus error, disabled, and prefix / suffix affixes.

Sizes

xSmall 24px · small 32px · default 40px · large 48px. Radius is 6px except large (8px). Type is 12 / 14 / 14 / 16px.

States

Default, focused (grey --ds-focus-border halo), filled, and disabled. Placeholder text is gray-700. Disabled fills the field gray-100 and mutes text to gray-700.

Error

Error swaps the ring to a red-900 hairline over a soft red-300 halo, with a message below in red-900 and the Geist error glyph.

Enter a valid email address.

Prefix & suffix

Bordered affixes sit on canvas (#fafafa), muted gray-700, divided from the field by a gray-alpha-400 hairline.
