Toast

Hand-rolled, portal-mounted notification anchored bottom-right; stacks up to three and self-dismisses after 5s. Surface + material-menu shadow + 12px radius on every variant. Four variants: default, success, warning, error — the message is text-only (no leading status icon); controls are a dismiss (X), an optional undo, and an optional full-width action row.
