Radio

Single-select control. A 16px hairline ring over the surface with an 8px inner dot — both share one --radio-color that shifts per state. The accent is neutral ink (Geist): no hue.

State Control Ring / dot colour
unchecked gray-700 ring · dot hidden (scale 0)
hover gray-900 ring · box fills gray-200
active gray-600 ring (press)
checked gray-1000 ring + dot
disabled gray-500 ring · dot hidden
disabled checked gray-500 ring + dot (disabled wins)
