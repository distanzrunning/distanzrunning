Checkbox

16 px box, 4 px radius, 1 px hairline. The accent is neutral ink — a checked box fills gray-1000 with a white check; there is no colour. Indeterminate keeps the surface box and shows a muted gray-700 dash. Label is 13 px, 8 px gap.

State Specimen Notes
unchecked Surface fill, gray-700 border.
checked Ink fill (gray-1000), white check glyph.
indeterminate Surface box, muted gray-700 dash.
disabled gray-100 fill, disabled border + text.
disabled · checked gray-600 fill (softer than active ink).

Box top-aligns to the first line of the label (items-start), so multi-line labels wrap cleanly beneath the text, not the box.
